Divyakant Agrawal

2026 SIGMOD Contributions Award

The SIGMOD 2026 Contributions Award goes to Divyakant Agrawal for his transformative leadership of SIGMOD, modernizing its publication and conference models, and expanding its global reach.

The homepage of Divyakant Agrawal can be found here: [https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~agrawal/](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~agrawal/)
