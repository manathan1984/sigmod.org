Besides the annual [SIGMOD/PODS conference](/conferences/), SIGMOD co-sponsors several other major conferences together with other ACM Special Interest Groups. Each conference is shared between its sponsoring SIGs, with the shares below.

| Conference | SIGMOD share | Other sponsors |
|---|---|---|
| [KDD](https://kdd.org/), ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining | 2.5% | SIGKDD 97.5% |
| [WSDM](https://www.wsdm-conference.org/), ACM International Conference on Web Search and Data Mining | 25% | SIGIR 25%, SIGKDD 25%, SIGWEB 25% |
| [SoCC](https://acmsocc.org/), ACM Symposium on Cloud Computing | 50% | SIGOPS 50% |
| [DEBS](https://debs.org/), ACM International Conference on Distributed and Event-Based Systems | 50% | SIGSOFT 50% |

SIGMOD members receive reduced registration at events sponsored or co-sponsored by SIGMOD. See also [meetings sponsored by SIGMOD](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/sponsorship-of-meetings/) and [events held in cooperation with SIGMOD](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/).
