There are four ACM SIGMOD Chapters across the world. Each of these chapters has a website (in alphabetical order) listed below.

-   [**China ACM SIGMOD Chapter**](https://china.acm.org/SIGMOD-China.html)
-   [**Japan ACM SIGMOD Chapter**](http://www.sigmodj.org)
-   [**Hellenic ACM SIGMOD Chapter**](https://acmsigmod.gr/)
-   [**Moscow ACM SIGMOD Chapter**](https://synthesis.frccsc.ru/sigmod/eng.html)
