## MentorNet

To ACM Student Members: If you think about any of the following questions:

-   What it’s like to work in industry?
-   What is graduate school all about, and is it for me?
-   How do I manage a career and a life?

ACM provides mentors to help you answer these questions.  Learn about the benefits of mentoring and find yourself a mentor at: [https://www.acm.org/articles/bulletins/2013/january/join-mentornet](https://www.acm.org/articles/bulletins/2013/january/join-mentornet)

## SIGMOD Dissertation Award

SIGMOD has established the annual SIGMOD Dissertation Award to recognize excellent research by doctoral candidates in the database field. Find more details and past winnere [HERE](/sigmod-awards/sigmod-jim-gray-doctoral-dissertation-award/ "The SIGMOD Jim Gray Doctoral Dissertation Award").

## Undergraduate Scholarship

SIGMOD offers undergraduate scholarship for undergraduates to attend SIGMOD conferences. The undergraduate scholarship includes travel stipend and free conference registration. Please see the web site of the current SIGMOD/PODS conference for more details.

The past scholarship recipients are listed under [Student Research Competition & Undergraduate Scholarship](/sigmod-history/past-awards/student-research-and-undergraduate/).
