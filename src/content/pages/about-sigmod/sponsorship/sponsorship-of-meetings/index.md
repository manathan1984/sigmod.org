SIGMOD can support a conference, symposium, or workshop in two ways: by holding it [in cooperation](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/) with SIGMOD, or by sponsoring it (like SIGMOD/PODS) or [co-sponsoring](/conferences/co-sponsored-conferences/) it. When SIGMOD sponsors a meeting, it takes responsibility for the meeting, including its finances and the choice of site.

## What Sponsorship Adds

A sponsored meeting gets all the [benefits of in-cooperation status](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/#benefits). In addition:

-   SIGMOD and ACM provide financial security for the meeting.
-   The organizers can ask the SIGMOD Executive Committee to publish the proceedings in SIGMOD *Record*.

## Requirements

A sponsored meeting must meet all the [requirements for in-cooperation meetings](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/#requirements), plus two more:

1.  **An ACM budget.** The meeting is budgeted under ACM’s sponsorship rules, using ACM’s standard budget form. A share of the income goes to ACM for its services, such as publicity, mailing lists, and producing the proceedings.
2.  **Close work with SIGMOD.** The SIGMOD Executive Committee approves the program chair, the program committee, and the budget, and the meeting is expected to break even. Regular SIGMOD-sponsored meetings are run by the Executive Committee or by a steering committee that reports to it.

Meetings held together with the SIGMOD/PODS conference have additional requirements.

## How to Apply

Apply the same way as for [in-cooperation status](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/#applying), with three differences:

-   Write to the [SIGMOD Chair](mailto:chair_sigmod@acm.org) and [Vice Chair](mailto:vc_sigmod@acm.org) **at least 11 months before the meeting**, instead of 10.
-   Add a list of the meeting’s other funding, such as other sponsors and grants.
-   After the Executive Committee’s informal approval, submit ACM’s Technical Meeting Request Form (TMRF) for a *sponsored* meeting. SIGMOD gives its official approval once ACM has approved the TMRF. For meetings held regularly, ACM can give a preliminary approval early.

Details of ACM’s process are in ACM’s [conference planning resources](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/volunteer-resources/conference-planning/table-of-contents).
