SIGMOD recognizes the best demonstration at the annual conference. There is usually one award for each demo session, but this varies by conference.

### 2026

**Best Demo:**

Demonstration of Vega-Video: Incorporating Video into the Grammar of Interactive Graphics  
Dominik Winecki; Arnab Nandi

**Honorable Mentions:**

RETRO: A Visual Tool for Understanding Cardinality Estimation Errors For Database Education  
Yani Zhang, Bingying Zhai, Hui Li, Sourav Bhowmick
