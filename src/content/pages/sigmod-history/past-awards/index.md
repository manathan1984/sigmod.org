SIGMOD has given several awards and student programs that are no longer active. Their recipients are kept here as part of SIGMOD's history. For the awards given today, see [Awards & Honors](/sigmod-awards/).

| Award | Years | Recognised |
|---|---|---|
| [Special Service Award](/sigmod-special-service-award/) | 1997 | Volunteer efforts for the database field |
| [Student Research Competition](/sigmod-history/past-awards/student-research-and-undergraduate/#student-research-competition) | until 2023 | Undergraduate and graduate student research, presented at SIGMOD |
| [Undergraduate Scholarship](/sigmod-history/past-awards/student-research-and-undergraduate/#undergraduate-scholarship) | 2000–2015 | Undergraduate students attending the SIGMOD/PODS conference |
