This logo, designed by Jun Yang of Stanford University, was the winning entry in a contest for a new SIGMOD logo held in 1998.

<h2 id="using-the-logo">Using the SIGMOD logo</h2>

You may use the ACM SIGMOD logo on:

-   your personal, academically oriented web page, hosted by your institution;
-   the website and publicity of an event [sponsored by SIGMOD](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/sponsorship-of-meetings/), [co-sponsored by SIGMOD](/conferences/co-sponsored-conferences/), or [held in cooperation with SIGMOD](/about-sigmod/sponsorship/conferences-symposia-and-workshops-held-in-cooperation-with-sigmod/). Use it only once the approval is confirmed, or add “(pending)” next to it until then.

When you use it:

-   Use one of the official files below, and do not modify it in any way: no recolouring, stretching, cropping or added text.
-   Link the logo to [www.sigmod.org](https://www.sigmod.org).

Any other use needs explicit permission from one of the [SIGMOD officers](/about-sigmod/) (Chair, Vice-Chair or Treasurer).
