<p class="lead">SIGMOD is the ACM community for data management: researchers, engineers, practitioners and students who build and study the systems, theory and tools for managing data. Membership keeps that community running.</p>

<p><a class="btn-join" href="https://campus.acm.org/public/gensigqj/gensigqj_control.cfm?promo=QJSIG&amp;offering=414&amp;form_type=SIG">Join SIGMOD at ACM</a></p>

You can join SIGMOD without being an ACM member. Membership is renewed each year through ACM.

## Why join?

SIGMOD sponsors the annual SIGMOD/PODS conference, publishes PACMMOD and SIGMOD Record, recognises the field's best work through its awards, and supports students and early-career researchers. All of it is organised by volunteers and funded in part by members. Joining is the simplest way to support the community and to have a say in it.

## Benefits of membership

-   **SIGMOD Record**, the quarterly bulletin of the community, and access to SIGMOD content in the ACM Digital Library.
-   **Reduced registration** at the SIGMOD/PODS conference and at other events sponsored by or held in cooperation with SIGMOD.
-   **A voice in SIGMOD**: members elect the SIGMOD officers and can volunteer for committees, reviewing and organising.
-   **Community news** about calls for papers, awards and nominations, and events.

See [Membership](/membership/) for the full list.

## For students

Student membership is available at a reduced rate. Student members are eligible for **SIGMOD awards**, such as the [Jim Gray Doctoral Dissertation Award](/sigmod-awards/sigmod-jim-gray-doctoral-dissertation-award/), and for **student travel grants** to attend the SIGMOD/PODS conference. Calls for travel grants are posted on each year's [conference website](/conferences/).

## How do I join?

-   [Join SIGMOD online](https://campus.acm.org/public/gensigqj/gensigqj_control.cfm?promo=QJSIG&amp;offering=414&amp;form_type=SIG) through ACM. Choose student membership if you are a student.
-   Consider [joining ACM](https://www.acm.org/membership/join) as well. ACM members get SIGMOD membership at the member rate, plus ACM's own benefits.
-   Questions about membership or renewals go to [ACM Member Services](https://www.acm.org/about-acm/contact-us).
