The year 2011 marked the 30th edition of the PODS conference. To honor this, an anniversary event was held together with the conference in Athens, Greece. This page contains some of the material that was presented at the PODS 30th Anniversary Event.

At the 30 Anniversary Event, talks were given by

-   Maurizio Lenzerini ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-lenzerini.pdf "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Maurizio Lenzerini"))
-   Moshe Vardi ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-vardi.pdf "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Moshe Vardi"))
-   Ron Fagin ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-fagin.ppt "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Ron Fagin"))
-   Jeffrey Ullman ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-ullman.ppt "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Jeffrey Ullman"))
-   Serge Abiteboul ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-abiteboul.pptx "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Serge Abiteboul"))
-   Victor Vianu ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-vianu.ppt "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Victor Vianu"))
-   Frank Neven ([slides](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/pods30-neven.pdf "PODS 30th Anniversary Slides Frank Neven"))

The talk by Frank Neven presents a data analysis, some of which can also be found in a [paper](https://sigmod.org/publications/sigmod-record/1109/pdfs/09.openforum.ameloot.pdf) which appeared in SIGMOD Record.

-   [The 3-clique community of PODS co-authors](https://sigmod.hosting.acm.org/sigmodnewwp/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/cliques.pdf "The 125-node 3-clique community"). More is discussed [here](https://sigmod.org/publications/sigmod-record/1109/pdfs/09.openforum.ameloot.pdf).
