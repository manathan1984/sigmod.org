The ACM announced its 2016 Distinguished Members.  Those with ties to the database community include [Sanjay Madria](http://awards.acm.org/award_winners/madria_5537987.cfm), [Laks Lakshmanan](http://awards.acm.org/award_winners/lakshmanan_3719473.cfm), and [Yufei Tao](http://awards.acm.org/award_winners/tao_0950279.cfm). Congratulations to all!
