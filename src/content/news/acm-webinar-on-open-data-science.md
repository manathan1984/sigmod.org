### Overview

**Title:** Embracing Open Data Science in Your Organization

**Date:** Wednesday, September 07, 2016

### Summary

([**Click here for starting time at your location**](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?msg=Embracing+Open+Data+Science+in+Your+Organization&iso=20160907T12&p1=179&ah=1))

Large organizations wanting to leverage the amazing technology being developed in open source for data science in the Python, R and Spark ecosystems struggle to modernize their traditional analytic stack.

Anaconda, the leading Open Data Science platform, is built on open source technology to fulfill the enterprise needs of Data Scientists and their colleagues, including Business Analysts, Developers, DevOps and Data Engineers. Anaconda leverages the open source innovation technology while providing enterprise features around collaboration, security, authentication, and deployment.

In this webinar, we’ll present the Anaconda platform and its foundational open source packages: Conda for package and environment management, Bokeh for interactive data visualization, Dask for parallel computing, Numba for high performance Python, and Datashader for large scale visualizations. We’ll discuss the workflow of Data Scientists from exploring and building machine learning models to collaborating in a team and deploying.

With the Anaconda Platform, it has never been easier to embrace and leverage Open Data Science.
